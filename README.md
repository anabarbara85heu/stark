## 🎯 What It Does
- Select any text on your screen (in a browser, email, document, etc.)
- Press a keyboard shortcut (like a hotkey)
- The text is instantly rewritten—cleaned up, rephrased, or improved
- Changes happen completely on your Mac, with no internet connection needed

## 💡 How to Use
1. Install the app (follow the download steps above)
2. Open it from your menu bar (top of your screen)
3. Select some text in any program
4. Press your chosen hotkey (usually set to Option+Space)
5. The rewritten text appears immediately

## 🔒 Privacy Guarantee
- Everything runs on your device
- No data is ever sent to the internet
- Your text never leaves your Mac
- No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking

## 🖥 System Requirements
- Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, or newer) or Intel
- macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later
- At least 8 GB RAM
- About 2 GB free disk space for the AI model

## ⚙️ Features

## 🔧 Troubleshooting
- **App won't open:** Ensure you've downloaded the complete ZIP, extracted fully, and that your Mac allows apps from unidentified developers (check System Settings > Privacy & Security)
- **Hotkey not working:** Click the app icon in your menu bar, go to Preferences, and reassign the hotkey
- **Slow rewriting:** The first rewrite may be slow as the AI model loads. Subsequent uses are faster.
